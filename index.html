About

I make operations boring, on purpose.

I'm a DevOps and Platform Engineer at Ole Solutions, where I run client infrastructure on Yandex Cloud: provisioning, access control, DNS and firewall configuration, deployment validation and live incident response.

Alongside that, I built an automation pipeline that finds bugs in open-source infrastructure tools, drafts Go fixes, and submits them upstream only after human review. Most fixes turn silent failures and panics into clear, actionable errors.

I hold an M.S. in AI & Cloud Computing and a B.Tech in Computer Science & IT from Lobachevsky State University (top 10% of class in both), and I write about cloud and DevOps fundamentals on Hashnode.
